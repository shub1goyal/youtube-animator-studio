# YouTube Thumbnail & CTR Engineering Blueprint

A scientific framework for generating 8% to 15%+ Click-Through Rates (CTR) in the animated history and educational documentary niche.

---

## 1. The Psychology of the History Thumbnail

Viewers on the YouTube homepage browse with their eyes in less than 1.5 seconds. They do not read titles first; they scan thumbnails for **emotional tension, recognizable symbols, and extreme curiosity**.

### The 3-Second Thumbnail Test
If a viewer looks at your thumbnail for 1 second at mobile scale (a 120x68 pixel display), can they immediately answer:
1. Who is involved? (e.g. Napoleon vs. Jefferson)
2. What is the emotional conflict? (Greed vs. Shock; Treachery vs. Panic)
3. What is the central object of desire? (A pile of cash, a disputed territory, a crown)

If the thumbnail has more than 3 distinct focal points, it fails the test.

---

## 2. The 3-Color Contrast Rule

High-CTR animated thumbnails strictly limit their dominant palette to 3 high-contrast colors:

1. **Dominant Background Color (60%)**:
   - Deep muted tones that recede: Navy Blue (`#0f172a`), Dark Parchment Sepia (`#3f2e18`), or Storm Slate (`#1e293b`).
2. **Subject Foreground Color (30%)**:
   - Clean, readable skin tones and iconic national uniform colors (French Royal Blue `#1d4ed8`, British Crimson `#b91c1c`, American Continental Navy `#0369a1`).
3. **Accent Hook Color (10%)**:
   - An electric, high-saturation color reserved **only** for the catalyst or focal point:
     - Neon Yellow (`#fde047`): Dollar signs, gold coins, crown glow.
     - Fire Red (`#ef4444`): Sale tag, laser eyes, battle markers.
     - Electric Cyan (`#06b6d4`): Glowing territory borders.

## 3. High-Converting Layout Templates

### Template A: The Historical Face-Off
- **Left Side**: Protagonist or instigator with smug / scheming expression (e.g., Napoleon holding a contract).
- **Right Side**: Opponent with extreme shocked / sweating expression (e.g., Jefferson with jaw dropped).
- **Center**: The disputed object (A glowing map of Louisiana with a giant "$15M" tag).
- **Lighting**: Bright rim light (white/cyan) tracing the outer edges of the character silhouettes to separate them from the dark backdrop.

### Template B: The Macro Catastrophe
- **Background**: Zoomed-out map with giant red arrows engulfing an empire.
- **Foreground**: Single monarch or general looking directly into the camera with hands on head in panic.
- **Overlay**: Bold 2-word text badge: "WRONG MOVE!" or "FATAL MISTAKE".

---

## 4. Text on Thumbnails: Rules & Pitfalls

- **Rule 1: Maximum 3 Words**: If your thumbnail needs a whole sentence, the visual design has failed.
- **Rule 2: Don't Duplicate the Title**: The thumbnail and title must complement each other, not repeat.
  - *Title*: How America Bought Louisiana for $15M
  - *Thumbnail Text*: "WORST DEAL EVER?" or "$15M (90% OFF)"
- **Rule 3: Heavy Black Stroke**: Always wrap thumbnail text in a 12–16px solid black outline or heavy drop shadow so it remains legible against any background.
- **Rule 4: Avoid the Bottom Right Corner**: YouTube's timestamp badge (e.g. `17:31`) covers the bottom right corner. Never place text or faces there!
