# Sample Production Bible: "The $15M Louisiana Heist"

An end-to-end production blueprint demonstrating the complete scripting, visual staging, sound design, and viral packaging methodology.

---

## Channel Concept & Positioning
- **Niche**: High-octane historical storytelling & animated geopolitical explainers.
- **Brand Identity**: 100% human-crafted 2D vector animation with cinematic cartography, dry humor, and character slapstick.
- **Competitor Benchmarking**: Matches the visual charm of *OverSimplified* and the pacing of *History Matters*, but introduces 4K cinematic lighting, 2.5D parallax, and broadcast-mastered audio.

---

## Packaging (CTR Engineering)

### Title Formulas
- **Primary Title**: How America Bought 1/3 of a Continent for $15 Million 🤯
- **Alternative A (Character-Driven)**: Napoleon’s Biggest Blunder: The Louisiana Disaster
- **Alternative B (Curiosity-Driven)**: The $15M Real Estate Deal That Shocked The World

### Thumbnail Strategy (The 3-Element Rule)
1. **Focal Point Left**: Napoleon Bonaparte chibi character in iconic bicorne hat, sweating profusely and holding a giant neon-red sale tag: **"$15M (90% OFF)"**.
2. **Focal Point Right**: Thomas Jefferson character wearing powdered wig with huge shocked anime-style eyes, holding a bag overflowing with dollar signs.
3. **Background**: Glowing map of North America with the giant middle territory lit up in glowing royal blue.
4. **Color Palette**: High-contrast triad: Royal Blue (`#1d428a`), Fire Engine Red (`#e53e3e`), Gold/Parchment (`#f5e6cb`).

## Shot-by-Shot Timeline (First 45 Seconds)

### Scene 1: The Impossible Deal (0:00 - 0:08)
- **Audio (VO)**: "In 1803, President Thomas Jefferson sent two diplomats to Paris with ten million dollars to buy a single port city... and they accidentally bought eight hundred thousand square miles."
- **Visuals**:
  - Starts tight on map of North America. Glowing blue electric Mississippi River flows down to New Orleans.
  - Two chibi diplomats in powdered wigs board a tiny sailing frigate on the East Coast.
  - Camera whip-pans across the Atlantic to Paris.
- **SFX**: Ocean wind ambience, ship bell clink, fast whoosh on camera whip-pan.
- **Music**: Energetic, inquisitive harpsichord and pizzicato strings (`-18dB`).

### Scene 2: Napoleon's Secret Ambition (0:08 - 0:18)
- **Audio (VO)**: "The man across the table? Napoleon Bonaparte. Now, Napoleon was busy trying to conquer literally everything, and he wanted a grand colonial empire in the Americas."
- **Visuals**:
  - Napoleon character stands on a map of Europe, chest puffed out, military medals shining.
  - He unrolls a giant blueprint map of North America, grinning smugly.
  - An animated speech bubble pops up with an imperial crown.
- **SFX**: Regal trumpet fanfare (half-second), paper unroll rattle, comedic slide-whistle on crown pop.

### Scene 3: The Haiti Disaster (0:18 - 0:28)
- **Audio (VO)**: "There was just one tiny problem: Napoleon’s entire American strategy hinged on the Caribbean island of Saint-Domingue—modern day Haiti."
- **Visuals**:
  - Camera zooms rapidly into the Caribbean. Saint-Domingue glows on the satellite map.
  - French troop silhouettes land on the beaches.
  - Sudden mosquito icons swarm the screen; French soldiers get cartoon "X" eyes and fall over like dominos.
- **SFX**: High-pitched buzzing mosquito sound, slapstick bowling pin crash sound as soldiers fall.

### Scene 4: The Cash Crisis & Backstab (0:28 - 0:38)
- **Audio (VO)**: "Fifty thousand French troops were wiped out by yellow fever and a relentless revolution. With no island base and Britain threatening war in Europe, Napoleon was broke."
- **Visuals**:
  - Split screen: On the left, a British king and Napoleon shake hands in front of a treaty, while both hide daggers behind their backs.
  - On the right, Napoleon looks into an empty French treasury chest; an animated cobweb and moth flies out.
- **SFX**: Dagger unsheathe metallic shimmer, sad trombone note, soft moth flutter.

### Scene 5: The Offer They Couldn't Refuse (0:38 - 0:45)
- **Audio (VO)**: "So when the Americans asked to buy just New Orleans, Napoleon smiled, pulled out the entire map of central North America, and said: 'How about the whole thing for fifteen million?'"
- **Visuals**:
  - Napoleon slaps a giant retail sale tag onto the Louisiana territory.
  - A modern silver briefcase of cash slides across the table into Napoleon's hands.
  - Diplomats' jaws drop to the floor (cartoon stretch easing).
- **SFX**: Cash register "cha-ching!", heavy table thud, cartoon spring jaw stretch sound.
