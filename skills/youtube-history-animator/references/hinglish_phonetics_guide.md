# Hinglish Phonetics & Pronunciation Guide for Gemini TTS (Voice: Charon)

When using Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (or any multilingual neural TTS model) with voice `Charon`, writing conversational Hindi words in standard Roman script often leads to westernized mispronunciations (e.g., the silent "e" rule turning "kare" into "care", or "bheje" into "beige").

This guide provides the exact transliteration and phonetic rules to ensure natural, colloquial, energetic Indian-accented speech.

---

## 1. The Core Phonetic Substitution Rules

### Rule 1: The Silent "E" Neutralizer
In English, a word ending in "e" either mutes the "e" or lengthens the preceding vowel. In Hindi/Hinglish, words ending in 'e' have the `/eː/` sound (like "say" or "day").
- **Do NOT write**: `kare`, `bheje`, `chale`, `mile`, `dekhe`, `apne`
- **DO write**: `karay`, `bhay-jay`, `chalay`, `milay`, `day-khay`, `up-nay`

### Rule 2: Diphthongs & Nasalization
- **"Mein" / "Hain"**: Standard English TTS reads "mein" like German "mine" or "main".
  - Transliterate to: `mayn` or `mehn`.
  - "Hain" -> `hayn`.
- **"Bhai" / "Bhaiyya"**:
  - Transliterate to: `bhaa-ee` or `bhaiya` -> `bhaa-ee-yaa`.
- **"Paisa" / "Paise"**:
  - Transliterate to: `py-saa` and `py-say`.

### Rule 3: Syllable Hyphenation for Fast Words
Multilingual neural models articulate compound and colloquial words with much higher accuracy when syllables are separated by hyphens:
- `khareedna` -> `kha-reed-naa`
- `khareedne` -> `kha-reed-nay`
- `socha` -> `so-chaa`
- `chahiye` -> `chaa-hi-yay`
- `jugaad` -> `joo-gaad`
- `lafda` -> `luff-daa`
- `locha` -> `low-chaa`
- `faayda` -> `faa-y-daa`
- `doston` -> `dhos-ton`
- `shuru` -> `shoo-roo`

## 3. Hinglish Scriptwriting Tone: The "3-T" Formula

To achieve 70%+ retention on YouTube Shorts with Hinglish:
1. **Tadkaa (Spice & Humor)**: Use casual street metaphors ("Napoleon bhaiyya ka dimaag kharab ho gaya", "Ek costly subscription scam ban gaya").
2. **Timing (Micro-Pauses)**: Insert short hyphens, exclamation points, and question marks to force the TTS engine to take natural breathing pauses between punchlines.
3. **Transition**: Seamlessly switch between Hindi conversational connectors and English technical/historical terms ("Treaty sign hui", "Westward expansion set ho gaya", "Deal of the century").
