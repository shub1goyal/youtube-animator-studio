## 2. Key Safeguards for AI-Powered Channels

1. **Defeating the "Reused Content" Flag on AI Voices**:
   - YouTube human reviewers ban faceless channels that combine generic AI scripts with Midjourney slideshows.
   - Your defense is the **custom 2D vector animation pipeline**: original chibi character puppet rigs, animated cartography, and Trim Paths. YouTube evaluates this as original craftsmanship.
2. **AI Voice Naturalization**:
   - Raw, robotic TTS flags reviewers. Running voiceover through the broadcast mastering chain (-14 LUFS long-form / -12 LUFS shorts, highpass 80Hz, presence EQ, dynamic compression) and layering tactile foley makes the audio sound broadcast-grade.
3. **Synthetic Media Disclosure (YouTube Studio)**:
   - Check **"NO"** to the altered/synthetic content prompt during upload. YouTube explicitly states that clearly unrealistic 2D cartoons, vector graphics, and animations are exempt.
4. **First 15 Seconds Safety Filter**:
   - Zero profanity, zero graphic trauma words ("slaughter", "massacre", "blood"). Lead with curiosity and diplomacy.
