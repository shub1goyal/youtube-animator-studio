# YouTube Advertiser-Friendly Guidelines for Historical & Animation Channels

A practical rulebook for keeping historical videos fully monetized (Green Dollar Icon) under AdSense and YouTube Partner Program guidelines.

---

## 1. The Core Policy Triad: Violence, Sensitive Events, and Language

Historical storytelling inevitably encounters wars, weapons, assassinations, treaties, and political revolutions. How you visually and linguistically depict these events dictates whether you get full ads, limited ads (Yellow Icon), or demonetized.

### Category 1: Violence & Battle Depictions
- **The Golden Rule**: The focal point of the video must **never** be graphic gore, dismemberment, or prolonged agony.
- **Why 2D Animation Is the Ultimate Monetization Shield**:
  - Depicting a historical battle with archival photos of corpses often triggers automated YouTube vision scanners and age-restricts or yellow-icons the video.
  - Depicting the same battle with 2D cartoon characters using slapstick conventions (e.g. spiral eyes, "X" eyes, falling over like bowling pins, crossed swords on a map, puffs of cartoon smoke) is classified as **clearly unrealistic animation** and earns **100% Green Icon eligibility**.

| Content Type | Green Icon (Full Ads) | Yellow Icon (Limited Ads) | Red Icon (Demonetized / Strike) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Battlefield Casualties** | Cartoon figures knocked down; map troop counters disappearing | Archival footage showing blood or non-gory bodies | Close-up graphic gore, mutilated corpses, decapitation |
| **Executions (e.g., King Louis XVI, Anne Boleyn)** | Sound effect (guillotine whoosh/slice off-screen) or character crown falling into a basket with comedic bounce | Depicting real historical woodcuts with explicit blood | Realistic graphic video or photo of beheading |
| **Weapons Handling** | Historical flintlocks, cannons, muskets, swords shown in animated/educational context | Weapons pointed directly at the camera in threatening modern manner | Instructions on manufacturing homemade firearms or explosives |

## 2. The First 7–15 Seconds Rule (The AdSense Crawler Gateway)

YouTube’s automated content moderation algorithms run an aggressive scan on the first 7 to 15 seconds of video and audio:
- **No Profanity**: Do not use strong profanity (F-words, S-words) in the first 7 seconds. Mild profanity ("damn", "hell") later in the video is allowed, but keeping educational content PG-13 maximizes CPM.
- **No Graphic Trauma**: Do not open the video with screams, gunshots, or words like "massacre", "blood bath", or "suicide".
- **Best Practice Opening**: Lead with curiosity, a paradoxical fact, or a geopolitical dilemma ("In 1803, two diplomats made the biggest mistake in real estate history...").

## 3. Sensitive Historical Events & Controversial Issues

Topics like slavery, the Holocaust, colonial exploitation, or recent geopolitical wars require extreme editorial care:
1. **Educational Context (EDSA Defense)**:
   - Frame every discussion around verified historical analysis, economic factors, and documented geopolitical treaties.
   - Use objective, documentary tone without glorifying perpetrators or expressing prejudice against protected groups.
2. **Visual Metaphors Over Graphic Realism**:
   - In Reference Video 2, when discussing the enslaved workforce in Saint-Domingue (Haiti) powering Napoleon's sugar economy, the creators used clean stick-figure icons holding sugar sacks and coffee beans on a satellite island map. This conveyed the historical reality clearly and humanely without triggering graphic depiction flags.
3. **No Hate Speech / No Historical Revisionism**:
   - Never justify atrocities or deny documented historical events.

---

## 4. How to Handle a False Yellow Icon Flag

Because automated bots scan metadata and transcripts, history videos are sometimes falsely flagged for words like "war" or "defeat":
1. **Self-Certification (Ad Suitability)**: Fill out YouTube's self-certification questionnaire accurately during upload.
2. **Upload as Unlisted First**: Always upload videos 24–48 hours before public release as `Unlisted`. If an automated yellow icon appears, it gives you time to request a human review before launch.
3. **Request Human Review**: Requesting a human review on an educational animation video almost always results in a reversal to Green Icon within 24 hours because human reviewers immediately recognize the non-graphic cartoon nature of the content.
