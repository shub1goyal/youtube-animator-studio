## 1. Understanding YouTube's Real Monetization Policy (2024–2026)

YouTube's official policy guidelines do **not** prohibit AI scriptwriting or AI voice synthesis. YouTube's policies (specifically Section 4 of the YouTube Partner Program guidelines) target:
1. **Reused Content**: Content repurposed without adding significant original commentary or value.
2. **Repetitious Content**: Mass-produced, programmatically generated content that offers no unique identity or narrative thread.

### The "AI Slop" Trap That Gets Channels Banned:
- Script: Generic ChatGPT prompt ("Tell me about the Roman Empire").
- Voice: Robotic, unedited free TTS (e.g. robotic TikTok voice or flat robotic tone).

- Sound: Dry, no background music, no sound effects, no pacing.
- **Outcome**: Immediate rejection by human reviewers for "Reused Content".

### Pillar 3: Studio Broadcast Mastering Chain
Raw TTS audio sounds dry and computerized. Our automated mastering chain applies:
- **80Hz High-Pass Filter**: Cleans sub-bass mud.
- **Parametric EQ**: -2.5dB at 320Hz (removes boxiness) and +1.8dB at 4.2kHz (adds vocal presence).
- **Dynamic Compression (3.5:1)**: Levels out peaks and adds radio-grade punch.
- **Loudnorm Limiting**: Normalizes to `-14.0 LUFS` (Long-Form) or `-12.0 LUFS` (Shorts).
- **Sound Effects Layering**: Layering tactile foley (whooshes, pops, map rolls) and ducking background music by `-18dB` under the voice completely masks any synthetic artifacts.
