# Public Domain & Copyright-Safe Asset Guide for History Creators

A curated repository of 100% legal, commercial-use friendly sources for historical maps, archival imagery, music, and sound effects.

---

## 1. Historical Maps & GIS Vector Data

Using modern copyrighted maps (like Google Maps screenshots or proprietary atlas scans) can lead to copyright strikes. Always source from public domain or permissive open-license repositories:

1. **Natural Earth Data (`naturalearthdata.com`)**:
   - **License**: Public Domain (CC0).
   - **Assets**: 1:10m, 1:50m, 1:110m global vector layers (coastlines, land, rivers, historical disputed borders) and raster shaded relief.
   - **Best For**: Importing into QGIS or Adobe Illustrator to build custom animated vector territories.
2. **Library of Congress Geography and Map Division (`loc.gov/maps`)**:
   - **License**: Vast majority published before 1928 are in the Public Domain worldwide.
   - **Assets**: Authentic antique maps from the 16th to early 20th centuries (e.g., 1803 Louisiana Purchase original treaty maps, Napoleonic battle plans).
3. **David Rumsey Historical Map Collection (`davidrumsey.com`)**:
   - **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial (check individual items for commercial availability) or public domain pre-1928 originals.
   - **Assets**: Over 150,000 high-resolution historical maps.
4. **USGS Historical Topographic Maps (topoView)**:
   - **License**: Public Domain (US Government work).
   - **Assets**: Detailed topographic surveys and regional riverways.

## 2. Archival Art & Historical Portraiture

Paintings and portraits created before 1928 (or where the creator died more than 70 years ago) are in the **Public Domain**.

1. **Wikimedia Commons (`commons.wikimedia.org`)**:
   - Check the licensing tag: Look for `{{PD-Art}}` or `{{PD-old-70}}` or `{{PD-US-expired}}`.
   - **Best For**: Historical portraits of Napoleon Bonaparte, Thomas Jefferson, King George III, Queen Victoria, etc.
2. **The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access (`metmuseum.org/art/collection`)**:
   - **Filter**: Check the "Open Access" box. Over 406,000 high-resolution images are released under CC0.
3. **National Portrait Gallery (UK & US)**:
   - Archival historical portraits of global monarchs, statesmen, and military commanders.

## 3. Commercial-Safe Music & SFX

YouTube's Content ID bot automatically claims ad revenue if you use unauthorized music. Stick strictly to these licensed ecosystems:

### Royalty-Free & Commercial-Use Music
1. **YouTube Audio Library (`studio.youtube.com`)**:
   - **Cost**: Free.
   - **Safety**: 100% immune to Content ID claims. Hundreds of classical, cinematic, and orchestral tracks that fit historical epics.
2. **Musopen (`musopen.org`)**:
   - Public domain and Creative Commons classical music recordings (Beethoven, Mozart, Bach, Vivaldi, Chopin) perfectly suited for Renaissance, Napoleonic, and Victorian eras.
3. **Kevin MacLeod (`incompetech.com`)**:
   - The legendary soundtrack of YouTube comedy and history (used heavily by OverSimplified, History Matters, and Kurzgesagt).
   - **License**: Free with attribution under CC BY 3.0/4.0.

### Sound Effects (SFX)
1. **Freesound (`freesound.org`)**:
   - Filter specifically for **Creative Commons 0 (CC0 / Public Domain)** to avoid attribution requirements.
   - Ideal for: cannon blasts, musket fire, wooden ship creaks, galloping horses, quill pens, paper rustles, parchment unrolling.
2. **Sonniss GDC Archive (`sonniss.com/gameaudioarchive`)**:
   - Massive free professional foley libraries released annually with 100% royalty-free commercial licenses.
